## The Challenge

A homeowner decided to sell by owner, and the listing needed a home base of its own: a website that showed the house well on phones and desktops, met accessibility standards, and let the owner edit the content.

## Our Approach

We designed, built, and hosted a custom website for the listing at [4001mossy.com](https://4001mossy.com).

- **Design** -- A responsive custom design with a hero photo carousel with motion, an accessible full-screen photo viewer, and an embedded 3D tour.
- **Accessibility** -- Automated WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility checks run on every change before it can go live.
- **Previews** -- Every proposed change gets its own preview link before it goes live.
- **Platform** -- Fast global hosting on Cloudflare, security headers, search and social link previews, photos stripped of GPS and location data, and content the owner can edit.

## Results

The home was listed on the MLS in July 2026 and sold in under three months. The website was the listing's home base throughout.

The site stays online as a portfolio piece. The 3D tour has since been deactivated and is shown as a placeholder.
